# Code of conduct

We want Noised to be a welcoming project for everyone, whatever their background, identity or level of experience.

## Our standards

Do:

- be respectful and patient, and assume good intent;
- give and accept constructive criticism of ideas and code, not of people;
- credit other people's work;
- keep security discussions responsible: report vulnerabilities privately ([SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md)).

Do not:

- harass, insult or demean anyone, publicly or privately;
- use sexualized language or imagery, or make unwelcome advances;
- publish other people's private information without their permission;
- promote tokens, solicit investment or spam in project spaces;
- do anything else a reasonable person would consider inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Scope

This code applies in every project space (issues, pull requests, discussions, chats and events) and when someone represents the project in public.

## Reporting and enforcement

Report unacceptable behaviour to the maintainers at `security@noised.fun` with "Conduct" in the subject. Reports are handled promptly and confidentially. The maintainers may edit or remove contributions and warn, suspend or ban anyone who breaks this code, in proportion to the harm done.
